Kendama Star Cup Juggler

A warm toy-stall pendulum dexterity arcade: tilt the kendama, swing the red ball, catch cups, balance through wobble, collect star charms, and spike the finale before the lantern fades.

Best score 0 Best Ceremony — View prompt

Tutorial

  • Objective: Swing the ball, catch it in the requested cup, balance it, collect star charms, then spike the finale.
  • Movement: Tilt Handle left/right or drag the stage; timing matters more than speed.
  • Rhythm: Swing Ball builds momentum; Pull String lifts the ball; Cup Catch lands when the cup window glows.
  • Tricks: Toss Release transitions between cups; Balance Hold steadies a catch; Spike Catch needs low sideways speed.
  • Star Focus: When charged, preview ball arcs, catch windows, spike alignment, tension risk, and trick progress.
  • Pause/restart: Use P/R on desktop or visible Pause/Restart buttons on mobile.

Audio starts after you press Start; visual ball, string, cup, spike, balance, tension, star, and focus cues work if muted.

Controls: A/D or arrows tilt, X Swing Ball, Z Pull String, Space Cup Catch, Shift/K Spike Catch, T Toss Release, B Balance Hold, C Swap Cup, F Star Focus, P pause, R restart.
