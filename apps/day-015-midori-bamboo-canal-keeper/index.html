Tutorial

  • Objective: fill requested basins before drought, overflow, or time runs out.
  • Canals: tap a bamboo tile to rotate its openings clockwise.
  • Preview/lock: pale-blue arrows show likely flow; lock key pieces before pulsing.
  • Basins: match blue clear, green moss, or gold koi labels and icons.
  • Drought/overflow: dead ends raise overflow; sun cracks evaporate water and raise drought.
  • Moss Patch: spend tanuki charge to repair a cracked tile or slow evaporation.
  • Pause/restart: use P/R on desktop or the visible mobile buttons.
