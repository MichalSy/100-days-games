How to play

  • Objective: Fly through 3D bonsai rings, collect sap, and forge all three upgrades.
  • Movement: WASD/arrows or mobile drag steer through depth-separated rings.
  • Rings: green cools heat, gold builds score and combo.

  • Keys: Space/Shift focus glide, P pause, R restart, 1/2/3 upgrades.
