Tutorial

  • Objective: rebuild kohaku porcelain with clean kintsugi seams before lacquer skins over.
  • Select/slide: tap a shard or use Shard −/+ and Slide controls to move it toward the ghost bowl.
  • Rotate Yaw + Tilt Pitch/Roll: turn the curved shard until seam ghosts become gold.
  • Snap Shard: snap only when the gap is small; severe mismatches crack porcelain.
  • Brush Lacquer + Dust Gold: brush in the requested star order, then dust when the seam sparkles.
  • Place Clamp + Warm/Cool: stabilize fragile seams, warm for flow, cool to slow skin and bubbles.
  • Star Focus: spend charge to preview target ghosts, alignment hints, safe brush direction, and clamp risk.
  • Pause/Restart: P/R on desktop or visible buttons on mobile. Audio starts after Start; visual cues work muted.
