  • Day 012 3d: snow-lantern stacking with real 3D blocks, center-of-mass, warmth, and gust shielding.

Day 014 chooses another hybrid, but it must feel mechanically and visually different from Day 013: paper layers and fold geometry replace rain lanes and shelter circles. It should use real layer/depth rules — mountain folds, valley folds, paper thickness, tear stress, crane launch paths, and overlap order — not just decorative isometric art.

Recent screenshot and visual variety notes to avoid repeating:

  • Day 011 used pale rose/ivory textile surfaces, a kimono-shaped grid, stamp trays, and right-side motif buttons.
  • Day 012 used dark winter blues, a central 3D snow pedestal/stack, block cards, and heavy bottom action buttons.

Day 014 should shift to a cleaner dawn-paper visual identity: layered translucent washi sheets on a green cutting mat, crease lines that glow when foldable, an origami crane avatar sliding along created ridges, torn-edge risk marks, ink seal collectibles, foggy cedar silhouettes outside the studio window, and a compact asymmetrical HUD that feels like a folding diagram notebook. Avoid umbrellas, rain streaks, puddle reflections, snow/candle/torii stacking, kimono cloth panels, stamp trays, cooking counters, wind ribbons, rail runners, or generic square boards.

Mode variety / 3D cadence

Inspection target: src/data/games.ts currently ends with Day 011 2d, Day 012 3d, and Day 013 hybrid. The latest generated mode streak is one hybrid; latest 2D streak is zero.

Mode decision: Day 014 is hybrid. It must be meaningful spatial hybrid gameplay, not decorative perspective:

  • Use a responsive static-browser 2D canvas/DOM hybrid with pseudo-3D paper-layer rendering.
  • Maintain at least three paper depth layers: base sheet, folded flap layer, and raised ridge/bridge layer.
  • Gameplay must depend on fold direction and layer order: a valley fold creates a trough path, a mountain fold creates a ridge bridge, overlap can hide or reveal seal nodes, and too many folds on one region raises tear stress.
  • The crane path must follow fold geometry and change when the player folds/unfolds paper.
  • Portrait mobile is the default supported layout; no landscape gate.

Design

  • Objective: Fold mist-soft washi paper into safe ridges, troughs, and bridges so a small origami crane can travel from the start mark to ceremony gates, collect ink seals, and avoid tearing the sheet before the dawn bell rings.
  • Win condition: Complete three authored folding chapters — First Crease, Cedar Bridge, and Dawn Crane Flight — while reaching 2800 points to trigger “Kiri Thousand-Fold Blessing”. After Thousand-Fold Blessing, continue into endless folding commissions.
  • Lose condition: Tear stress reaches 100%, three crane feathers break from bad paths, the dawn timer expires during a commission, or the crane falls into an unfolded gap too many times.
  • Core loop:
  1. Start on a title/menu screen with Day 014 badge, mode badge “hybrid”, public route /kiri/, best score, best Thousand-Fold time, tutorial, prompt link, and a large Start button.
  2. Show a portrait-friendly cutting-mat playfield with a translucent washi sheet, visible crease candidates, start mark, ceremony gate, ink seals, tear-stress marks, and a tiny origami crane resting at the start.
  3. A commission card requests fold goals, for example: “Make 2 valley paths, 1 mountain bridge, collect 3 coral seals, keep tear stress under 55%.”
  4. Player taps or drags along crease candidates to select a fold line, then chooses Mountain Fold, Valley Fold, Unfold, or Reinforce.
  5. Folded flaps animate over/under the base sheet, revealing or covering seal nodes; ridge/trough arrows show how the crane will travel.
  6. Press Launch Crane to let the crane glide along the current fold geometry. The crane collects ink seals, crosses bridges, slides down troughs, and reaches the gate if the path is coherent.
  7. Tear wisps appear when the same paper region is folded too often. Reinforce with rice-paper tabs to reduce stress, but tabs are limited and lower combo reward.
  8. Completing a commission stamps the sheet with a vermilion seal, awards points, restores one feather if needed, and unlocks the next fold pattern.
  9. Pause/restart remain available throughout.
  • 15+ minute play-value strategy:
  • Save best score, fastest Kiri Thousand-Fold Blessing time, longest perfect-fold streak, most seals in one launch, highest endless commission, and collected origami mastery badges in localStorage.
  • Include three authored chapters:
  • First Crease: broad sheet, two obvious crease candidates, one valley path, no instant tear, teaches select fold / mountain vs valley / launch.
  • Cedar Bridge: three layers, one overlap reveal, two ink seals behind flaps, first tear-stress tradeoff, first reinforce tab.
  • Dawn Crane Flight: multi-step route with mountain bridge plus valley trough, moving fog gap, hidden seal revealed by unfolding, stricter stress and timer.
  • Deterministic Day 014 seed varies crease candidates, seal placement, fog gaps, gate position, commission chip order, paper grain weakness, and endless constraints while keeping the opening fair.
  • Mastery badges: finish First Crease with zero stress, collect 12 ink seals, trigger Thousand-Fold Blessing under 190 seconds, complete a commission without Reinforce, launch a crane through 4 fold features, finish an endless commission with all feathers.
  • Strategic scoring rewards planning: preview the route before Launch, choose mountain vs valley based on crane direction, unfold at the right time to reveal seals, save Reinforce for weak grain zones, and chain seals in one clean flight.
  • Endless mode after Thousand-Fold Blessing adds denser crease options, stricter stress caps, fog gaps that drift between launches, hidden seals, and faster dawn timers without shrinking touch controls.
  • Difficulty scaling:
  • 0-45 seconds: two to three crease candidates, large fold buttons, broad gate, no tear penalty during first guided fold.
  • 45-115 seconds: overlap/reveal rules, first weak-grain zones, first hidden seal, three fold choices.
  • 115-190 seconds: multi-layer bridge/trough route, moving fog gap, stricter stress cap, shorter launch preview window.
  • 190+ seconds/endless: denser fold networks, weaker paper regions, higher seal requirements, same readable controls.
  • Keep mobile fair: crease lines and fold handles must be thick, crane/gate/seals readable at 390x844, commission text short, 56px+ main controls, no tiny hazard required for survival.
  • Scoring/rewards:
  • Correct fold matching commission: +65 points times combo tier.
  • Crane crosses a ridge/trough feature safely: +70 points.
  • Ink seal collected during launch: +120 points.
  • Gate reached with stress below target: +360 points and +10% Reinforce charge.
  • Perfect commission with no broken feathers: +480 points.
  • Kiri Thousand-Fold Blessing: +940 points and endless folding commissions unlock.
  • Wrong fold direction: combo reset, stress +8%, path preview turns amber.
  • Crane fall or torn region: feather -1, stress +12%, launch ends.
  • Over-reinforcing: safe but reduces current combo multiplier.

Controls and layout

  • Desktop:
  • Mouse click/tap: select crease line, seal/gate tooltip, action button, start/pause overlay button, or prompt link.
  • Mouse drag: draw along a crease candidate to select it; drag-to-fold should preview mountain/valley direction if practical.
  • Arrow keys or WASD: move fold focus among crease candidates.
  • M: apply Mountain Fold.
  • V: apply Valley Fold.
  • U or Backspace: Unfold selected crease if allowed.
  • F: Reinforce selected weak region if charged.
  • Space or Enter: Launch Crane / start from menu.
  • P: pause/resume.
  • R: restart current run.
  • Mobile/touch:
  • Tap a thick crease line to select it.
  • Use large Mountain, Valley, Unfold, Reinforce, Launch, Pause, and Restart buttons.
  • Optional drag along a crease line may select/preview, but tap-select plus large buttons is mandatory for reliability.
  • Tap seal/gate chips for short explanations.
  • No virtual joystick. Interaction is fold selection, fold action, route preview, launch, reinforce, pause/restart.
  • Mobile layout/orientation:
  • Default target is portrait phone play around 390x844; do not require landscape.
  • Top: compact notebook-ribbon HUD with score, best, feathers, stress, chapter, combo, and time. Avoid repeating Day 013’s wide rain stat chips; use folded-corner paper tabs and one stress brush mark.
  • Below top: commission card with fold chips, seal target, stress cap, and progress ticks.
  • Center: cutting-mat/paper playfield with washi sheet, selected crease, fold layers, crane route preview, seals, gate, fog gap, and stress wisps.
  • Bottom: selected-crease helper plus large fold action controls. Controls must not cover the crane route or fold lines.
  • Tutorial must fit on the first portrait screen; optional details may be collapsible but objective, mountain/valley difference, route preview, Launch Crane, stress/reinforce, pause/restart must be visible.
  • Requests must combine text, icons, line styles, and labels so hue alone is not required.
