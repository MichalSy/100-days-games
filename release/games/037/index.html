Tutorial

  • Objective: Scoop requested goldfish patterns into the bowl before the paper tears.
  • Move: drag slowly; fast movement raises ripples, turbulence, and paper wetness.
  • Tilt Net: switch Flat, Left Edge, Right Edge, or Nose Down; approach from behind.
  • Dip/Lift: lift when a requested fish overlaps the net and the timing ring is small.
  • Safety: Swap Poi before tearing; avoid maple leaves and high turbulence.
  • Festival Focus: when charged, slow fish and show safe arcs, requested highlights, and tear warnings.
  • Pause/Restart: use visible buttons, or P/R on desktop.

Audio starts after you press Start; visual ripple, paper, and order cues work if muted.
