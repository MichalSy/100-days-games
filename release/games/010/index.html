Kaze Windbell Atelier

Rotate and tune hanging windbells so gust ribbons ring the requested notes.

Best score: 0 · Grand Chime: --

Tutorial

  • Objective: route teal gust ribbons through the requested Blue LOW, Amber MID, and Silver HIGH bell chips.
  • Select: tap/click a bell, then rotate, tilt, or tune with the large buttons or keyboard.
  • Gust routing: wind catches only bells whose mouth faces the incoming ribbon and whose height/depth is close.
  • Pitch: use Pitch, Z/X/C, or 1/2/3 to set Blue low, Amber mid, or Silver high.
  • Hazards: missed gusts raise storm; crows can snatch charms and crack rattled bells.
  • Resonance Pulse: when charged, Space/Enter slows gusts, scares crows, and previews the next good bell.
  • Pause/restart: use P/R or the visible buttons.
