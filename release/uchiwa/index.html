Tutorial & Objective

  • Objective: Dye requested fan sectors, protect white breeze gaps, and dry the washi before pigment bleeds.
  • Selection: Step with Sector −/+ and Band −/+ or tap large fan wedges.
  • Pigment: Choose indigo, coral, or saffron; Brush Pigment when the stencil aligns.
  • Stencil: Rotate wave, goldfish, lantern, and firefly masks onto requested sectors.
  • Paper safety: Fold Edge redirects wet flow, Blot Cloth rescues bleed, Fan Dry locks clean edges.
  • Kaze-Dry Focus: Charged by clean stencil matches; slows the workshop and previews bleed/dry paths.
  • Pause/restart: P/R on desktop or visible buttons during play.
