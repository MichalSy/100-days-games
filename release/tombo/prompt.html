Design

  • Objective: Guide a shimmering dragonfly through rice-paddy dewlines, skim ordered dew beads, perch on reeds to restore wing energy, dodge frogs and spider silk, and complete three morning flight commissions before the sun dries the field.
  • Win condition: Complete three dewline commissions — First Dew Skim, Reed Gate Spiral, and Sunrise Terrace Crossing — while reaching 6100 points to trigger “Tombo Dawnline Blessing”. After the banner, continue into endless terrace flights.
  • Lose condition: Three wing hearts are lost, wing energy reaches 0 during a hazard hit, sun-dry meter reaches 100%, the dragonfly is caught by frog tongues three times, six dew beads evaporate uncollected, or the Sunrise commission receives two wrong-order dew skims.
  • Core loop:
  1. Start on a title/menu screen with Day 047 badge, mode badge “2D”, public route /tombo/, best score, best Dawnline Blessing time, tutorial, prompt link, audio note, and a large Start button.
  2. Show a portrait-friendly rice-paddy flight stage. The dragonfly is a clear player sprite near the center-lower field; ordered dew beads glimmer along curved rice rows; reed perches and hazards are readable at phone size.
  3. A dewline card requests goals such as: “Skim blue-gold dew 1→4, perch on the right reed, clear one silk snag, and keep sun-dry below 45%.”
  4. Player steers the dragonfly with direct drag/touch or arrow controls. Momentum matters: sharp turns bleed wing energy, while smooth banked curves maintain combo.
  5. Wing Burst gives a short forward impulse and lifts above water, useful for crossing row gaps or escaping a frog warning, but overuse drains wing energy.
  6. Skim Dew lowers the dragonfly close to the water/rice blade line for a brief window. It collects active dew beads only when heading and distance are right; skimming too early splashes and loses speed.
  7. Perch Reed snaps to a nearby highlighted reed perch if aligned and slow enough, restoring wing energy and revealing the next dewline segment. Perching on a decoy reed wastes time.
  8. Quick Turn flips heading through a tight arc; it can preserve combo after a wrong approach but raises silk-snag risk if used near webs.
  9. Dodge Frog performs a vertical sidestep/hover when a frog tongue warning arc flashes. Timing it during the amber warning pulse gives score; spamming it drains focus.
  10. Clear Silk cuts one spider-silk snag only when the dragonfly is close and wing angle matches the strand. Failed clears bounce the dragonfly and fray a wing heart.
  11. Field Bell sends a gentle ripple through the paddy, briefly slowing dew evaporation and stunning one frog if used on a blue bell pulse.
  12. Tombo Focus, charged by clean dew chains, smooth bank turns, perfect perches, and timed dodges, overlays ordered dew routes, safe skim windows, frog-tongue arcs, silk tension, gust drift, perch alignment, evaporation risk, and the safest next action.
  13. Completing a commission stamps a rice-paper flight talisman, restores one wing heart if below max, awards points, shifts the terrace layout, and unlocks tighter rows, moving dew beads, diagonal gusts, faster sun-dry, chained frog warnings, fragile silk, and longer ordered dewlines.
  14. Pause/restart remain visible throughout.
  • 15+ minute play-value strategy:
  • Save best score, fastest Tombo Dawnline Blessing time, longest clean dew chain, highest smooth-bank combo, most perfect reed perches, most timed frog dodges, highest endless terrace number, fewest wrong-order dew skims, best no-focus flight, and collected flight-talisman badges in localStorage.
  • Include three authored commissions:
  • First Dew Skim: broad rice rows, four obvious dew beads, one safe reed perch, slow sun-dry, guided first Steer, Wing Burst, Skim Dew, and Perch Reed. No heart penalty for the first tutorial splash.
  • Reed Gate Spiral: curved row route, first Quick Turn, first frog warning, first Field Bell pulse, one silk snag that requires Clear Silk, and a right-side perch recovery.
  • Sunrise Terrace Crossing: full field with two ordered dewlines, diagonal gust drift, required Tombo Focus preview, frog and silk combo hazard, sun-dry target below 50%, and no wrong-order dew skims in the final chain.
  • Deterministic Day 047 seed varies dew bead order, rice-row curve, water drift, reed perch timing, frog warning phase, silk strand angle, gust side, wing-energy drain, evaporation pace, Field Bell rhythm, Tombo Focus charge, and endless constraints while keeping opening seconds fair.
  • Mastery badges: complete First Dew Skim with zero splashes, trigger Dawnline Blessing under 285 seconds, finish Reed Gate Spiral with every perch perfect, clear Sunrise Crossing with sun-dry below 35%, chain eight dew beads without wrong order, dodge three frog arcs on amber pulse, and complete a commission without Tombo Focus.
  • Strategic scoring rewards graceful movement: bank before bursting, skim only when aligned with the dewline, perch before wing energy is desperate, use Quick Turn away from silk, dodge frogs on warning pulse, ring Field Bell before evaporation spikes, and save Tombo Focus for crossing/spiral routes.
  • Endless mode after Dawnline Blessing adds longer dewline orders, tighter row gaps, faster evaporation, crossing frog arcs, silk clusters, variable gusts, decoy reeds, bonus sunrise talismans, and higher combo multipliers without shrinking touch targets.
  • Difficulty scaling:
  • 0-45 seconds: slow dragonfly, broad dew collection radius, large reed perch, one safe frog far from route, visible route hints.
  • 45-150 seconds: curved routes, first Quick Turn, frog warning, Field Bell, and one silk clear.
  • 150-285 seconds: required Tombo Focus, diagonal gust, two dewline orders, frog+silk hazard pairing, tighter skim timing.
  • 285+ seconds/endless: faster dry meter, moving dew beads, more hazards, longer routes, same readable controls.
  • Keep mobile fair: dragonfly, dew beads, rice rows, reed perches, frog arcs, silk strands, gust ribbons, commission card, helper, focus/energy/sun HUD, and action buttons must be readable at 390x844; primary touch buttons should be 52px+ where possible and never below 44px; no tiny survival-critical dew beads.
  • Scoring/rewards:
  • Smooth bank through a rice row without losing combo: +110 points times combo tier.
  • Correct ordered Skim Dew: +260 points and Tombo Focus charge.
  • Perfect Perch Reed recovery: +320 points and wing energy restore.
  • Wing Burst through a row gap without splash: +210 points.
  • Quick Turn preserves chain after a near miss: +190 points.
  • Dodge Frog on amber warning pulse: +340 points and combo protect.
  • Clear Silk with correct wing angle: +300 points.
  • Field Bell slows evaporation during blue pulse: +230 points.
  • Complete commission before sun warning: +1100 points and restore one wing heart if below max.
  • Perfect no-splash commission: +1500 points.
  • Tombo Dawnline Blessing: +3700 points and endless terrace flights unlock.
  • Wrong-order dew skim, splash, silk bounce, frog catch, missed perch, or sun-dried bead: combo reset and wing/sun penalty.

Controls and layout

  • Mouse click/tap on the stage: set steering target, select dewline bead, perch, silk, frog warning, or explainable stage chip.

  • Arrow keys or WASD: bank/steer.
  • Space or Enter: Skim Dew.
  • Shift or X: Wing Burst.
  • P or E: Perch Reed when aligned.
  • Q: Quick Turn.
  • D: Dodge Frog.
  • C: Clear Silk.
  • B: Field Bell.
  • F: Tombo Focus when charged.
