# Day 005 Postmortem - Yume Lantern Railrunner

## Result
Implemented one Day 005 game from `prompts/day-005.md`: a static mobile-first Three.js/WebGL 3D lane runner where the player rides a lantern tram through dream rails, switches lanes, collects dream tickets/star fragments, avoids cracked rails/paper moths, uses Lucid Bell, and chases Yume Dawn Arrival plus endless-loop scoring.

## What worked
- The mode cadence stays strong: after Day 004 returned to `2d`, Day 005 is real `3d` with a perspective camera, depth-separated rails, lane movement, gates, collectibles, hazards, fog, parallax background, and 3D event timing.
- The public Japanese-word alias is `/yume/`, while immutable archive output lives under `release/games/005/`.
- Prompt archive was copied exactly to `release/games/005/prompt.md` and rendered as semantic HTML in `release/games/005/prompt.html` plus the `/yume/prompt.html` alias.
- Desktop and 390x844 mobile smoke checks passed: title/menu, tutorial, start, lane controls, Lucid Bell key path, pause/resume, restart, no console errors, and screenshot capture.
- A Three.js standalone route pitfall appeared again: `three.module.min.js` imports `three.core.min.js`. The missing module caused `/yume/assets/three.core.min.js` 404 and prevented `game.js` from installing event listeners. Adding `three.core.min.js` beside every vendored Three.js module fixed the route.
- Mobile visual QA found the tram was initially too low and partly hidden by bottom controls. The game was adjusted by lifting/scaling the tram group so the sprite remains visible while keeping 56px+ controls.

## Generated-image inspection notes
Image generation produced all requested source artwork, then optimized playable copies were prepared for the app, immutable release, and `/yume/` alias.

- `lantern-tram-source.png` / `lantern-tram.png`: clear centered Japanese lantern tram silhouette, no visible text or watermark, generous crop margins, strong warm lantern readability. It has a plain dark background and a front-facing/up-track travel direction; in-game lane changes tilt/slide the tram left/right consistently. The source includes rail base art below the tram, so the playable texture is used as a decorative sprite over simpler Three.js tram geometry; final mobile QA confirmed it reads at game scale.
- `dream-rail-sky-source.png` / `dream-rail-sky.png`: portrait dream-rail background with open central corridor, moon/origami constellation detail above, and rail/lamp framing toward the lower sides. No text/watermark. In-game a dark overlay/fog keeps Three.js rails, tickets, gates, and hazards readable over it.
- `yume-icons-source.png` / `yume-icons.png`: dream ticket, star fragment, lucid bell, lantern heart, moon gate, cracked rail, paper moth, dawn bell, safe rail, and sleep/moon meter icons are visually distinct on a dark background. No watermark. The moon-gate icon contains tiny decorative pseudo-glyph marks on the frame, but no meaningful readable text; acceptable at HUD size.
