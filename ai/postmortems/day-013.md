## Remaining notes

- The route smoke exercises the primary controls and verifies the hybrid shelter/rain scene, but does not grind long enough to trigger Ame Moonlit Procession naturally. The progression path is implemented through chapter goals and score thresholds.
- The generated mascot and icon sheet are not alpha cutouts. This is acceptable for their current framed/menu usage, but future prompts should keep asking for transparent sprites when direct compositing or moving sprites are required.
- Concurrency with the implementation subagent can leave mismatched partial files if the controller also recovers manually. Future runs should prefer either waiting for the implementation summary before controller fixes, or clearly isolating controller asset preparation from app HTML/CSS/JS writes until the subagent is done.
