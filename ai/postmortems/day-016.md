## What shipped

- Public route: `/ryu/`.
- Immutable archive: `release/games/016/`.
- Mode: mobile-first real `3d`, chosen after Day 015 `2d` to keep the series spatial and avoid drifting into flat board puzzles.
- Core play: select a 3D clay vessel ring, widen/narrow it toward a target silhouette, smooth wobble, carve dragon-scale grooves, apply ash-blue glaze bands, fire the kiln, and use Bellows/Vent to keep heat in the safe band.
- Mobile layout: portrait-first 390x844 route with compact ceramic HUD, commission card, central 3D pottery wheel scene, ring chips, selected-ring helper, and 44px+ sculpt/fire controls.
- Prompt archive: `prompts/day-016.md` copied exactly to `release/games/016/prompt.md` and `release/ryu/prompt.md`; rendered semantic prompt pages at both aliases.

## Generated-image inspection notes

- `ryu-apprentice-source.png` / `ryu-apprentice.png`: readable upright pottery apprentice in indigo clay apron with bowl, bamboo rib tool, and tiny ember dragon charm. Centered crop, stable pivot, strong small-size silhouette, no visible text or watermark. It has a pale clay background rather than true alpha transparency; acceptable because it is menu/helper art, not a moving collision sprite.
- `ryu-kiln-studio-source.png` / `ryu-kiln-studio.png`: portrait warm mountain pottery studio with dragon kiln, shelves, bowls, tools, mountain view, and open pottery-wheel center. The first optimized copy had transparent side padding after contain-resize; it was corrected with cover-crop/no-alpha output. Final asset has no black side bars, no visible text or watermark, and remains suitable behind the 3D scene after the game overlay darkens the center.
- `ryu-icons-source.png` / `ryu-icons.png`: generated source was landscape and contained a fake checkerboard-like neutral background. The optimized runtime copy was alpha-cleaned/composited onto a pale clay background so no baked checkerboard remains. The final sheet keeps distinct ring profile, widen/narrow tools, smoothing sponge, dragon-scale stamp, glaze brush, ember flame, vent fan, bellows, crack warning, patience tile, and Ryu seal. Caveat: some icon details are painterly, so runtime primarily uses these as decorative/UI asset references while critical gameplay controls remain text-labeled.
- `screenshot.png`: final desktop route capture shows non-empty gameplay with readable HUD, commission card, 3D vessel/wheel, ghost target silhouette, selected ring halo, silhouette percentage, helper, ring selector, and sculpt controls. Separate 390x844 mobile screenshot confirmed portrait usability and visible large controls.
