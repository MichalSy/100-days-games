# Day 004 Postmortem - Hikari Firefly Cartographer

## Result
Implemented one Day 004 game from `prompts/day-004.md`: a static mobile-first 2D path-drawing arcade puzzle where the player draws glowing route strokes to guide colored fireflies into matching lantern constellations before dawn.

## What worked
- The mode cadence remains healthy: Day 003 was `3d`, so Day 004 returned to `2d` with a different interaction model (direct path drawing / light routing) rather than another vehicle-steering game.
- The game is portrait-first and playable at a 390x844 phone viewport. HUD, request labels, Moon Pulse, pause, and restart controls remain visible with large touch targets.
- The public Japanese-word alias is `/hikari/`, while immutable archive output lives under `release/games/004/`.
- Prompt archive was copied to `release/games/004/prompt.md` and rendered as semantic HTML in `release/games/004/prompt.html` plus the `/hikari/prompt.html` alias.
- The launcher and git-tracked cron prompt were tightened after preflight showed stale skill/toolset references; future scheduled runs now request the same curated maximum-practical skill/toolset context used here.

## Generated-image inspection notes
Image generation produced all requested source artwork, then optimized RGBA copies were prepared for sprite/icon assets.

- `firefly-keeper-source.png` / `firefly-keeper.png`: cute upright firefly cartographer charm with clear forward/upright orientation, centered silhouette, generous crop margins, no visible text or watermark. Source has a plain black background; optimized playable PNG has alpha transparency and reads well at the in-game corner scale. Control-to-motion risk is low because the keeper is decorative and the moving fireflies are procedural glow sprites.
- `moon-garden-source.png` / `moon-garden.png`: portrait shrine garden background with a wide open center path, no text/watermark, and strong side framing. Vision inspection confirmed the center remains readable on the 390x844 crop; the game overlays a subtle dark wash so fireflies, strokes, lantern requests, and hazards remain visible.
- `hikari-icons-source.png` / `hikari-icons.png`: red/blue/gold/violet/rainbow fireflies, lantern, moon pulse, shadow cat, wind, puddle, ink, and dawn map icons are visually distinct on a dark background with no text/watermark. Optimized sheet has alpha transparency. Hazards use cat/wind/puddle silhouettes and are not easily confused with collectible fireflies.
