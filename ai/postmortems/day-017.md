# Day 017 Postmortem — Kumo Silverweb Starcatcher

## QA summary

Day 017 was generated from the archived prompt at `prompts/day-017.md`. A fresh implementation subagent was dispatched with only the repo path, day number, archived prompt path, validation rules, and code-isolation rule. The controller generated and inspected Imagegen2 assets, assembled/reconciled the app/release/alias output from the archived prompt, and completed the end-to-end local validation, screenshot capture, Docker/static smoke, and deployment readiness in the controller session.

## Code-isolation confirmation

No previous game implementation source was intentionally opened, inspected, copied, diffed, grepped, or adapted during the Day 017 implementation/QA work. Previous days were used only through `src/data/games.ts`, `release/manifest.json`, prompts, postmortems, and screenshots for variety planning before `prompts/day-017.md` was written. Day 017 HTML/CSS/JS was written for `apps/day-017-kumo-silverweb-starcatcher/` from the archived Day 017 prompt and general browser/canvas game knowledge, then copied into `release/games/017/` and `/kumo/`.
