# Day 008 Postmortem - Mori Mosslight Seedkeeper

## Result

Implemented one Day 008 game from `prompts/day-008.md`: a static mobile-first Three.js/WebGL 3D moss-routing strategy arcade game. The player inspects a miniature forest-shrine board, selects and rotates raised moss/root tiles, places or recalls a temporary root bridge, routes glowing dew beads into requested seedling basins, manages drought/soot-mite pressure, and uses Mosslight Lantern while chasing Mori Mosslight Bloom plus endless forest-night scoring.
