## Remaining notes

- The route smoke exercises the primary controls and verifies the 3D scene, but does not grind long enough to trigger Yuki Grand Illumination naturally. The progression path is implemented through chapter goals and score thresholds.
- The generated icon sheet and mascot are not alpha cutouts. This is acceptable for their current framed/menu usage, but future prompts should continue asking for transparent sprites when direct compositing or moving sprites are required.
